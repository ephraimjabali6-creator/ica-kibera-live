# UX rules this site follows

1. **One clear next step per screen.** Every page ends with a primary action (Plan a visit, Reserve seats, Request prayer).
2. **Thumb-first on phones.** Bottom action bar (Visit · Watch · Prayer), 44–48px tap targets, full-screen menu.
3. **Fast on slow networks.** Self-hosted fonts, lazy images below the fold, CSS-only hero slideshow, route progress bar.
4. **Never lose what people typed.** Forms keep input on errors; checkout drafts survive refresh; offline banner explains what's happening.
5. **Errors say what to do.** Field-level messages next to the field, focus moves to the problem, no codes or jargon.
6. **Respect people.** Anonymous prayer allowed, "pastors only" option, cookieless analytics, no pop-ups beyond a one-time notice.
7. **Accessible by default.** Skip link, visible focus rings, alt text, aria-live status messages, reduced-motion support, AA contrast.
8. **Kenyan context.** Phone/WhatsApp first (07xx numbers normalised to +254), EAT times, Kiswahili welcome copy.
9. **Consistent brand.** White + ICA purple, the I/C/A stacked logo, real photos of our church — no stock imagery.
10. **Honest states.** Seat counts are live; closed or full events say so; the seat hold timer is visible.
